Questions

Good to know before you book.

Is this a men's salon or a ladies' salon?

Both. Wavelength is a unisex salon. One reviewer describes roughly two stylists on the men's floor and five or six on the women's. Men's haircuts, beard trimming and shaving sit alongside women's colour, keratin, facials and bridal work.

What are the opening hours?

Google shows the salon closing at 8:30 pm, and two business directories list a 10:00 am opening. Hours can change on public holidays, so call or WhatsApp to confirm before you travel.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Google lists appointments as required, and the salon has stated publicly that it works to prior appointments. Booking ahead is the safest option, particularly for colour and treatment work that runs long.

Where exactly is it?

1st Floor, P-62, CIT Road, above Maa Durga Chemist Shop, opposite ESI Hospital, CIT Scheme VII M, Kankurgachi, Kolkata 700054 — Plus code H9PR+9M.

Please note that some directories still list an older address at P-310 CIT Road, Phool Bagan. The salon has since moved to P-62. If a map sends you to the old location, call the salon on +91 62901 92038.

Are there other Wavelength branches?

Yes — three outlets across Kolkata: Kankurgachi, Ballygunge and Garia. The Ballygunge and Garia branches each hold a 4.6★ rating on Google. They are on separate numbers, so call the branch you actually want to visit — this page's number, +91 62901 92038, is Kankurgachi's.

How much do treatments cost?

The salon does not publish a price list online, so we cannot list prices here. Individual reviews mention package and combo offers, but these change and we have not reproduced them. Call the salon for current pricing.

What is the Wavelength Café?

An in-house café, Wavelength Cafe and Kitchen, serving mocktails and drinks during your appointment. Beverages are listed as an offering on the salon's Google listing, and several guests mention the drinks specifically.

Is the salon accessible?

Google lists a wheelchair-accessible entrance, gender-neutral toilets and a restroom. The salon is also listed as good for kids.
